About Me

I am Jeremy Cordero, a student at Celebration High School who enjoys learning how technology works and building things with it.

What I Like to Learn

I am especially interested in robotics, artificial intelligence, computer science, and electrical engineering. I like seeing how software and hardware work together, from code and circuits to machines that can sense and respond to the world.

STEM and Leadership

I take part in Science Olympiad, ACE, EnergyWhiz, and other school and community activities. These experiences let me solve problems, work with a team, and learn by trying new things.

I also founded Celebration Hack Club, a student technology club where students can explore programming and electronics through hands-on projects. I enjoy helping others learn and making technology feel welcoming to beginners.

Looking Ahead

My goal is to study engineering and work on innovative technology, especially robotics and intelligent systems. I hope to keep building projects, learning new skills, and creating things that are useful to people.
