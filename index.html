About Me

Hey, I'm Jeremy Cordero, a student at Celebration High School who's passionate about technology, engineering, and figuring out how things work. I'm especially interested in robotics, artificial intelligence, computer science, and electrical engineering, and I enjoy exploring how these fields come together to build smarter, more capable systems.
Outside the classroom, I spend a lot of time learning, experimenting, and working on projects. I enjoy programming, solving problems, participating in STEM competitions, and turning ideas into something real. I'm also involved in several organizations, including Science Olympiad, ACE, EnergyWhiz, and Embrace Celebration, where I've developed experience in leadership, teamwork, and helping others.
One of my biggest projects has been founding Celebration Hack Club, a student-led technology club focused on making programming, electronics, and engineering more accessible to students. I wanted to create a space where people could learn, experiment, and build things together, regardless of their experience.
Looking ahead, I hope to pursue engineering, particularly robotics and AI hardware. I'm fascinated by the intersection of software and physical systems, and I want to keep pushing myself to understand how technology works and what we can do with it. Ultimately, I want to build things that are not only technically interesting but also genuinely useful.
